# Stage 1: Introduction to Latin

## Overview
Welcome to the first stage of your Latin learning journey. This stage introduces you to the fundamental concepts and vocabulary that will form the foundation of your Latin studies.

## Learning Objectives
- Understand basic Latin pronunciation
- Learn essential vocabulary words
- Practice basic sentence structure
- Develop reading comprehension skills

## Key Concepts

### Latin Alphabet and Pronunciation
The Latin alphabet consists of 23 letters, similar to the English alphabet but without the letters J, U, and W. Each letter has a consistent sound, making Latin pronunciation more predictable than English.

### Basic Vocabulary
Essential words for beginners include:
- **Salve** - Hello
- **Vale** - Goodbye
- **Gratias** - Thank you
- **Sit** - Yes
- **Non** - No

### Sentence Structure
Latin follows a different word order than English. The basic structure is:
- Subject (Nominative case)
- Object (Accusative case)
- Verb (usually at the end)

## Practice Exercises

### Exercise 1: Vocabulary Matching
Match the Latin words with their English meanings:
1. Salve → Hello
2. Vale → Goodbye
3. Gratias → Thank you

### Exercise 2: Basic Sentences
Translate these simple sentences:
- "Salve, amice!" (Hello, friend!)
- "Gratias tibi ago." (I thank you.)

## Assessment
At the end of this stage, you should be able to:
- Pronounce Latin words correctly
- Recognize and use basic vocabulary
- Understand simple sentence patterns
- Read basic Latin texts with confidence

## Next Steps
After completing Stage 1, you'll move on to Stage 2, where you'll learn about noun declensions and more complex sentence structures.
